# Clientroom final handover: master index

This folder is the complete handover for the Volopay Founder's Office, Product and
Growth assignment. It is written from the actual implementation, not from plans.

## How to read this folder

- **Evaluator-facing** documents are safe to share with Volopay. They contain no
  secrets, no personal paths and no unverified claims.
- **Preparation** documents are for Anuvind. They contain learning material,
  interview answers and personal notes.

## Evidence status legend

- **Verified**: implemented and independently checked.
- **Implemented**: built and in use, not independently re-checked.
- **Pending**: not done, stated as such.
- **Unvalidated**: an assumption or hypothesis, not tested.
